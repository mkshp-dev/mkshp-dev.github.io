Indian History Everyday Uploader is a small, private publishing tool that uploads short videos to the YouTube channels owned by its operator, Mukund Shelake, including @indianhistoryeveryday ("This Day in Indian History").

What the app does

Every day the channel publishes one short video about a lesser-known event in Indian history that happened on that date. The videos are written, reviewed and rendered on the operator's own server. Once a video has been approved, this app:

  • signs in to the operator's own YouTube account with Google OAuth, after the operator grants permission on Google's consent screen;
  • uploads the approved video file with its title, description, tags and scheduled publish time, using the YouTube Data API;
  • reads back the upload status of those videos so the operator can confirm that each one was processed and published.

The app does not read, change or delete any other content, does not access other people's accounts, and is not offered to the public. It has no sign-up and no users other than the channel owner.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
